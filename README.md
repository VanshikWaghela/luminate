## 🌟 What is Luminate?  
Luminate is a user-friendly decision-support tool designed for students who are unsure about their next career step. Whether you're considering pursuing a **master's degree** or diving straight into the **job market**, Luminate offers tailored insights based on your profile to help you make informed decisions.  

---

## 🎯 How Does It Work?  
Simply provide your academic and professional details, and Luminate calculates two scores:  

1. **Composite Decision Score (Master's)**  
   Assesses your readiness for graduate studies based on key factors like GPA, work experience, GRE score, research papers, and university ranking preferences.  

2. **Job Suitability Score (JSS)**  
   Evaluates your suitability for the job market based on technical skills, project experience, soft skills, and adaptability.  

### ⚖️ The Results  
Luminate compares your scores and suggests whether you should:  
- Pursue a **master's degree**  
- Enter the **job market**
